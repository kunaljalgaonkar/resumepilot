Common questions
Does the free plan give me the same AI quality as Pro?
Yes. Every plan uses the same Claude Haiku model and the same prompts. The only difference is how many ATS analyses you can run — not the quality of the tailored resume.
What counts as an ATS analysis?
Each time you click "Evaluate Match" in the floating panel, that uses one analysis. Resume tailoring is always free and unlimited. The analysis is the optional match-score breakdown showing what's missing from your resume for that specific JD.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your account settings anytime. You keep Pro access through the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees, no questions asked.
Is my resume data private?
Your resume and job description text are sent to Claude for tailoring and then immediately discarded — not stored on any server. The only data we store is in your browser's local extension storage, which only you can access.
What is the application tracker?
Pro users get an automatically maintained log of every job they've applied to through ResumePilot — company name, role title, date applied, and which version of their resume was used. It's built from your real application activity, not manual entry.
